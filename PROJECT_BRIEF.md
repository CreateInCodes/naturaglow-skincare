# Project Brief: NaturaGlow Skincare Store

## 1. Store Overview
- **Store Name:** NaturaGlow
- **Domain:** naturaglowskincare.com
- **Niche:** Organic Skincare & Wellness
- **Target Audience:** Health-conscious shoppers & self-care enthusiasts looking for premium natural skincare.

## 2. Brand Vibe & Design System
- **Aesthetic:** Clean, minimalist, and modern luxury.
- **Color Palette:** Sage Green (`#4A6B5B`), Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`), Soft Gold (`#D4AF37`), Warm Neutral accents (`#F7F5F0`).
- **Typography:** Elegant sans-serif / serif headings with high readability.

## 3. Product Catalog (4 Flagship Products)
1. **NaturaGlow Hydrating Rose Face Mist** — ₹599 (Pure steam-distilled damask rose water with aloe vera & witch hazel)
2. **Vitamin C Glow Facial Serum** — ₹899 (With 15% Vitamin C, Kakadu Plum & Hyaluronic Acid for radiant skin)
3. **Deep Nourishing Kumkumadi Night Oil** — ₹1,299 (Traditional Ayurvedic gold saffron & sandalwood facial elixir)
4. **Green Tea & Matcha Clay Face Mask** — ₹699 (Detoxifying purifying clay mask with green tea extracts)
5. **Ultra-Hydrating Botanical Daily Sunscreen SPF 50** — ₹799 (Lightweight non-greasy broad spectrum sun protection)

## 4. Required Pages & Features
- **Home Page:** Hero banner with luxury imagery, announcement bar ("Free shipping on orders above ₹499"), featured collection, trust badges, customer reviews.
- **Shop / All Products Page:** Filter by category, sorting, search, quick view.
- **Product Detail Page (PDP):** High-res image gallery, key ingredients list, skin benefits, customer reviews and rating breakdown, add to cart.
- **About Us / Brand Story:** Clean narrative on organic sourcing and cruelty-free mission.
- **Ingredient Glossary:** Explanation of hero ingredients (Saffron, Vitamin C, Green Tea, Rose Water).
- **Contact & FAQ:** Dedicated support phone number, support email, interactive contact form, and expandable FAQ accordion.
- **Cart & Checkout Suite:** Interactive slide-over cart, promotional discount code input, simulated Indian Checkout (UPI QR code GPay/PhonePe, Cash on Delivery, Card/Netbanking), order success screen with itemized invoice.
